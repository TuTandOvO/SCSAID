# Style Profile — mapmyvisitors.com

## One-line vibe

Compact operational analytics: quiet white surfaces, thin grey structure, dense visitor facts, and contextual map detail that stays out of the way until requested.

## Palette and typography

- Canvas and surfaces: true white / near-white, separated by `#e4e5e7` hairlines.
- Ink: near-black for primary figures; medium grey for metadata.
- Accent: a conventional medium blue reserved for active controls and links.
- Status: red/orange dots communicate live or recent activity.
- Type: one neutral sans-serif with a tight product scale; large values are light, not decorative.

## Spacing, shape, and elevation

- Dense 10–20px panel padding and compact table rows.
- Square-to-2px corners and flat panels with borders rather than broad shadows.
- Information is grouped by operational purpose: summary metrics, map, timing, visitor history, and location tables.

## Interaction and motion

- Map detail appears contextually from a marker rather than occupying permanent space.
- Visitor summaries progressively disclose their visit timestamps.
- Motion is limited to status updates, hover/focus feedback, and disclosure transitions.

## Do-not-copy list

- MapMyVisitors logo, wording, visitor icons, flags, browser assets, tracking scripts, Bootstrap class names, and map data are not reused.
- scSAID keeps its self-hosted Leaflet/GeoJSON stack, typography, identity, and protected analytics data.
